GOBLET GOBBLERS: THE RULES

These rules are for this setting:
  - You need 4 in a row to win (WIN_LENGTH = 4).
  - You may move pieces that are already on the board (ALLOW_MOVES = True).


THE BOARD AND THE PIECES

1.  Two players play. Player 1 is Blue. Player 2 is Brown.
2.  The board has 4 rows and 4 columns, so it has 16 squares.
3.  One square is blocked. Nobody can ever put a piece there.
4.  There are three sizes of pieces: small, medium and large.
5.  Each player starts with 3 small, 3 medium and 3 large pieces. Then one piece of the same
    size is taken away from both players. So each player has 8 pieces, and both players have
    the same pieces.
6.  At the start of each game, the blocked square and the size that is taken away are picked
    at random.
7.  The board starts empty, except for the blocked square.
8.  The pieces you have not put on the board yet are called your reserve.
9.  Nothing is hidden. Both players can see every piece, even pieces covered by other pieces,
    and both reserves.


TAKING TURNS

10. A match between two agents has 2, 4 or 6 rounds. One round is one game. The course staff
    will announce the exact number. In every round, Player 1 goes first, and then the players
    take turns. The agents swap seats after each round, so each agent is Player 1 in half of
    the rounds. This way both agents get the same chance to go first.
11. On your turn you do exactly one of these two things:
      a. Put a new piece from your reserve on the board.
      b. Move one of your pieces that is already on the board to a different square.
12. You may move a piece even when you still have pieces in your reserve.


COVERING PIECES

13. A piece can go on an empty square.
14. A piece can also go on top of a smaller piece. This covers the smaller piece.
    A medium can cover a small. A large can cover a small or a medium.
15. A piece can never go on a piece of the same size or a bigger size.
    Nothing can cover a large.
16. You may cover your own pieces and your opponent's pieces.
17. One square can hold up to three pieces: a small at the bottom, then a medium, then a large.


MOVING PIECES

18. You can only move your own pieces.
19. You can only move a piece that is on top. A covered piece cannot move until the piece on
    top of it leaves.
20. The piece must go to a different square. Rules 13 to 15 apply there too.
21. When you lift a piece, the piece under it can be seen again.
22. A piece on the board never goes back to your reserve.


WINNING

23. Get 4 of your pieces in a straight line to win. The line can go across (a row), up and
    down (a column), or from corner to corner (a diagonal).
24. Only the pieces on top count. A covered piece does not count for anyone.
25. A line through the blocked square can never be finished.
26. Be careful when you lift a piece. If the piece under it gives your opponent 4 in a row,
    your opponent wins. This is true even if your move also gives you 4 in a row.
27. You are safe only if your opponent has no 4 in a row after your move. For example, the
    piece you lift can cover one of your opponent's pieces in that line.


HOW THE GAME ENDS

28. The game ends as soon as someone has 4 in a row.
29. Each player gets at most 50 turns, so 100 turns in total. If nobody has won after 100 turns,
    the game is a draw. A 4 in a row made on turn 100 still wins.
30. If it is your turn and you have no move you are allowed to make, you lose.
    (This does not happen in a normal game.)
31. Playing the same moves again and again does not end the game. Only the 100-turn limit does.


POINTS

32. A win gives 1 point. A draw gives half a point. A loss gives 0 points.
33. Rounds come in pairs. Both rounds of a pair start with the same board and the same pieces.
    Only the seats are swapped.


RULES FOR YOUR AGENT

34. Your agent has 2 seconds to choose each move.
35. Your agent gets a strike if it is slower than 2 seconds, picks a move that is not allowed,
    or has an error. The referee then plays a random move for it.
36. 3 strikes in one game are forgiven. The 4th strike loses the game.
37. In the tournament, if your agent takes more than 10 seconds for one move, it loses the game.
38. If your agent crashes, it loses the game.
39. Your agent has 30 seconds to get ready at the start of each game. If it is slower, or has an
    error while getting ready, it loses the game.
40. Your agent may use up to 500 MB of memory.
